Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Pick the way that suits you. Reports from the app are the quickest.

Report from the app (best)

In TestFlight, take a screenshot in URC Trumps and tap Share Beta Feedback. Or open the TestFlight app, choose URC Trumps, and tap Send Beta Feedback.

This goes straight to the developer with your device details.

Use the bug report form. You need a GitHub account.

    Questions

    Why isn’t a player in the game?
    Cards include players with at least 10 URC appearances who have a photo, plus every stat leader.
    How fresh are the stats?
    Stats are updated from the URC site nightly, and every few hours on match weekends. A new TestFlight build carries them.
    Does it need internet?
    No. Games run offline.
    Does it collect data?
    No. Read the privacy policy.
