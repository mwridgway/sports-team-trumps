Needs an iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Install Apple’s TestFlight app first, then open the beta link.

Pick a stat. Win the hand.

Every card is a real URC player with six stats from official URC match data since 2021/22: height, weight, tackles, tries, metres made and points.

  1. Pick your stat

    Choose the stat you think beats your opponent’s card.

  2. Reveal

    Both cards turn over. The higher number wins.

  3. Take every card

    Win the hand and you take both cards. Take the whole deck to win the game.

Draws go into the ruck

If the numbers match, both cards go into the ruck. The winner of the next hand takes the lot.

Three levels of computer

Play on Easy, Medium or Hard. Hard remembers the cards it has seen.

No.1 tackles

Leader cards

The player with the most of any stat gets a silver foil card with a crown. When you hold a leader card, you know which stat to pick.

Nick Timoney’s 1,047 tackles are the most in the URC, so his card is silver.

A file on every player

Tap Player file on any card to read the full story behind the numbers.
