# URC Trumps privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

URC Trumps is a personal, non-commercial card game built by Matthew Wridgway.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Games against the computer run entirely on your phone, and your settings (such as the computer's difficulty) are stored only on your device.

**Playing a friend:** when online games are added, the app will send the moves of that game to the game server so both phones can see them. Games are identified by a random room code and an anonymous device token, not by your name or Apple ID, and finished games are deleted.

**Player data:** player statistics and photos come from the United Rugby Championship's public website. Player background facts come from public sources and show where they came from.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/mwridgway/sports-team-trumps/issues
